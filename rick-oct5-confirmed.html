Tokyo: Monday, October 5

A morning at the market, an afternoon in art and shopping.

  • Duration: about 9 hours (5:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.)
  • Distance: mostly by taxi and train
  • Cost: included (teamLab and meals separate)

The day

  1. 5:30
    Toyosu Market: Tuna auction viewing deck Meet Yuuki at the check-in desk (Building 7, 3F Floor). The auction runs 5:45–6:25. You will see buyers bidding on tuna in real time. The market is cold and loud and smells like fish. Bring a sweater and comfortable shoes.

  2. 6:30–7:30
    Breakfast at Toyosu Market Walk the market stalls with Yuuki. Try fresh seafood donburi (rice bowl) or a quick breakfast set at one of the market shops. Tables and benches throughout.
  3. 9:00
    teamLab Planets Your premium pass tickets allow you to enter anytime between 8:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m., so 9:00 is ideal. Skip the ticket line and show your QR code at the information center. Plan to spend 1.5–2 hours inside. You will walk through immersive digital art installations: crystal universes, flowers falling from ceilings, light responding to your body.

  4. 10:30
    Tsukiji Outer Market Take a taxi from teamLab (about 10 minutes). Walk the stalls: fresh sashimi, tamago (egg), sushi rice snacks, dried seaweed. Yuuki will meet you here. Small shops sell ready-made sushi, salad rolls, and tea.
  5. 12:00
    Lunch: Ginza Sushi Masa Reservation for three under Yuuki's name. East Ginza Station, 3-minute walk. Sushi counter, intimate, omakase style (let the chef decide). About 45 minutes.

  6. 1:00–3:30 p.m.
    Ginza walk and shopping A short, slow walk near the restaurant. Stop at:
    • Itoya — stationery and notebooks
    • Mitsukoshi basement — sweets and souvenirs
    • Kimuraya — bread and pastries
    • Ginza Six rooftop — city views and Tsutaya bookstore
    • Nakamura Tougetsuan — matcha and sweets
    End at your hotel on foot (15 minutes from Ginza Six) or by taxi (5 minutes).
